+Website use and customer responsibilities+

Use the site lawfully and provide accurate contact and fulfilment details. Product pages and displayed stock are a guide to what is currently recorded; availability, options, and prices can change before the seller reviews a request. Do not treat an item as reserved or a price as agreed until the seller confirms it.

Checkout details create an order request. The seller reviews it and confirms the product, total, payment and fulfilment details. A payment reference, uploaded proof, or message sent to WhatsApp does not by itself confirm payment or acceptance.

Kenyan law provides consumer protections. Depending on the transaction, these can include rights to goods and services of reasonable quality, information needed to benefit from them, protection of health, safety and economic interests, and compensation for loss or injury. These terms do not remove rights or remedies that apply to your purchase. See the Constitution of Kenya and the Consumer Protection Act.

{% elif request.endpoint == 'public.payment_policy' %}
+Payment problems and next steps+

The checkout records a reference or optional proof as payment reported; it does not verify the transaction automatically. The store records verified payment separately.

  1. Keep the M-Pesa transaction message and order reference.
  2. If the reference is missing, incorrect, duplicated, or payment status has not changed, contact the store with your order reference and transaction reference.
  3. Do not send your M-Pesa PIN or other login credentials.

Do not pay again until you have checked the status with the seller.

{% elif request.endpoint == 'public.delivery_policy' %}
+Timing changes or delays+

The site does not publish a guaranteed delivery time. Agree the expected timing and handover details directly with the seller. Keep your phone available for updates. If arrangements change or are delayed, contact the seller using the Contact page before travelling or making alternate plans.

{% elif request.endpoint == 'public.returns_policy' %}
+Cancellation, exchange, or refund request+

Contact the store promptly through the Contact page with your order reference and request (cancellation, exchange, or refund). For an incorrect, damaged, or apparently defective item, report it within the 48-hour period stated above and describe the issue; photos may help the review. Wait for the seller to confirm the next step before sending an item back or expecting a refund.

A request starts a review; it does not automatically approve a return or refund. Refund requests are reviewed and recorded by the store, and no processing deadline is promised here. Applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

Quick answers

Does submitting checkout confirm my order?

No. It creates an order request. Wait for the seller to confirm the item, total, payment, and fulfilment.

What should I include when contacting the store?

Include your order reference and a brief description. Never send a password, PIN, or payment credentials.
