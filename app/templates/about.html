Who we are

Fashion should not have to cost a fortune.

FityStores is a merchandise shop focused on making everyday fashion essentials accessible, convenient, and affordable.

We bring together practical products and stylish finds for students and people in the surrounding neighbourhood.

FityStores is owned and operated by Oxy-Jen Tech.
