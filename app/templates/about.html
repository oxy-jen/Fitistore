Helpful answers

A few things to know

How do I place an order?

Open a product to check its details and available options, add it to your cart, then follow the checkout instructions shown on the site.

How can I check if something is available?

Check the product page for current stock and options. If its price or availability needs confirmation, contact the shop before paying.

How do collection and delivery work?

See the current delivery and collection information and the options shown during checkout. Contact the shop if you need clarification.

How do I get help with an order?

Use the Contact page. For an order update, use the site's order tracking page when you have the requested details.
