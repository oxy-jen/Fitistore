Deleting records

Download and check an export before each cleanup. Choose exactly one section per cleanup; after a section is deleted, that export is consumed, so download a fresh export before clearing another section. The export includes local product image files; selecting Products removes the catalog and its local image files. Selecting Orders clears every order in the export. Category cleanup is not date-filtered. Admin login, category definitions, and shop settings stay available. Cloudinary-hosted image files remain in Cloudinary; only local uploaded product images are removed.

Some linked records may be included by more than one selected group; each record is deleted only once.
