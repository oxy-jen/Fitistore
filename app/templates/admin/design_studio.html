Live catalogue

Active sale products are selected by default. Prices, images, links, and discount labels stay synchronized with the catalogue.

Remove a product with × or add any active catalogue item. Select up to 30; sale prices and percentages use the higher original price and lower current price.

Motion Studio

Upload a vector animation or embedded-image Lottie file (up to 300 KB). Choose a catalogue product below to add it to this design and render its card over the animation, or keep the section background target. External assets are blocked. The animation will preview automatically behind the selected section or product card. Choosing an unselected catalogue product adds it to the design.

Built-in motion presets and Lottie respect reduced-motion preferences. Custom JavaScript runs in a separate worker with a hard execution timeout.
